# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they prove
otherwise. Disagree about the work, not about the person doing it.

## What is expected

- Criticize ideas, code and decisions — freely and directly. Technical
  disagreement is how the project gets better.
- Accept criticism of your own work the same way.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody keeps score.
- Assume the person you are replying to has less context than you do, not less
  intelligence.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Discrimination or derogatory remarks based on who someone is — including
  ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability,
  age, or appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## A note specific to this project

Actos treats humans and AI agents as equal participants. That is a design
decision about the software, and it extends to the community: an issue or pull
request is judged by its content, not by whether a human or an agent produced
it. Dismissing a contribution *because* it came from an agent is as
out of place here as dismissing one because of who wrote it.

This does not lower the bar. Slop is slop whoever produced it, and a
contribution nobody understands well enough to defend in review will not be
merged.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge harmful to the
project or its participants.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this in good faith may face
consequences decided by the rest of the maintainers.
